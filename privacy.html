Privacy

This page explains how the current Empire Stogie Lounge website handles information when you visit it. The site is primarily an informational website for an adults-only 21+ cigar lounge.

Age confirmation

When you confirm that you are 21 or older, the website stores a simple age-verification flag in your browser’s local storage so the age screen does not need to appear every time. This confirmation is stored on your device and is not an account.

Information you submit

The current website does not include an account system, checkout, newsletter form, or contact form that collects personal information directly. If that changes, this notice should be updated before the new feature is used.

Third-party services

The website links to or loads content from services such as Google Maps, Google Fonts, Instagram, and Facebook. Those services may receive ordinary technical information associated with a web request, such as your IP address, browser information, or referring page, and they operate under their own privacy policies.

Analytics and advertising

The current site does not state that it uses an analytics or advertising tracker. If analytics, advertising pixels, or similar tracking technologies are added later, this notice should be updated to describe them.

External links

Links to Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps, and other outside services take you to websites that are controlled by those providers. Empire Stogie Lounge is not responsible for their privacy practices.

Questions

For questions about this website or the lounge, use Empire Stogie Lounge’s current Instagram or Facebook presence.
