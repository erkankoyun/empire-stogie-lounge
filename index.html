FIRST VISIT

Know the essentials.

A few quick answers make the first visit easier. For policies that can change, contact the lounge directly before arriving.

Message Empire Directions
Who can enter the lounge?

Empire is an adults-only 21+ tobacco lounge. A valid government-issued photo ID may be required.

What are today’s hours?

Current operating hours can change. Check Instagram or Facebook before traveling.

Where is Empire?

15 Park Ave, Suite 14, Clifton Park, New York. Use the map above or open Google Maps for turn-by-turn directions.

Can I ask about a specific cigar before I come?

Yes—inventory changes, so messaging the lounge is the best way to ask whether a particular brand, line, or size is currently available.

Do I need a reservation?

For seating, group visits, or special arrangements, message Empire before arriving so the lounge can give you the current policy.

What about outside cigars, beverages, parking, or other lounge policies?

Those policies can change and are not stated here without verification. Message Empire directly for the current answer before your visit.
