# Catalog A — verified 2026-10-16

9 selected products: Alec & Bradley (2), Foundation (3), U.S. Cohiba (2), Perdomo (2). `products.json` contains page sources and image source URLs for every product. All nine images are real manufacturer product photographs, downloaded locally; no generated photographs.

## Alec & Bradley
- Current manufacturer pages https://alecbradley.com/cigars/blind-faith and https://alecbradley.com/cigars/gatekeeper confirm Toro 6 x 52, blends, medium body and listed tasting notes.
- Blind Faith wrapper wording on official page is internally inconsistent: blend table says Honduran Trojes, tasting prose says Honduran Broadleaf. Catalog uses only unambiguous `Honduras (Trojes)`.
- Alec & Bradley press release reproduced at https://cigarweekly.com/cw-news/alec-bradley-blind-faith-featured-at-the-tpe explicitly says Blind Faith rolled at Raices Cubanas, Danli, Honduras.
- CRITICAL: Gatekeeper moved from La Alianza Dominican Republic to Tabacos de Oriente Danli Honduras in July 2021. https://www.cigaraficionado.com/article/alec-bradley-gatekeeper-production-moving-from-dominican-republic-to-honduras quotes brand statements. Catalog uses Honduras and Plasencia's Tabacos de Oriente, despite many outdated retailer Dominican listings.

## Foundation
- Official https://foundationcigarcompany.com/charter-oak-ct-shade/ confirms Toro 6 x 52, Connecticut Shade wrapper, Sumatra binder, Nicaraguan filler, mild-medium strength. It does not state the wrapper's growing country; do NOT add Ecuador or USA automatically. Factory marked `Not disclosed` because selected official page does not identify factory. Handmade/Nicaragua corroborated by selected-vitolа retailer https://www.neptunecigar.com/cigars/foundation-charter-oak-connecticut-toro . Tasting notes expressly labeled retailer Small Batch: https://www.smallbatchcigar.com/charter-oak .
- Official https://foundationcigarcompany.com/the-tabernacle-ct-broadleaf/ confirms Toro 6 x 52, CT Broadleaf, Mexican San Andres binder, Esteli/Jalapa/Jamastran filler, full strength. Foundation release reproduced https://cigardojo.com/2016/07/foundation-cigar-co-announces-the-tabernacle/ confirms A.J. Fernandez Esteli factory. Toro tasting notes labeled Smokingpipes retailer: https://www.smokingpipes.com/cigars/foundation-cigar-company/moreinfo.cfm?product_id=333832 .
- Official https://foundationcigarcompany.com/olmec/ confirms Toro 6 x 52, Mexican San Andres, Jalapa binder, Esteli/Jalapa filler, full strength, cacao/dark espresso/spice. Official asset filename explicitly `olmecmaduro-Toro-6x52-1.png`. Selected Toro's handmade/box-pressed/Esteli A.J. Fernandez corroborated https://cigarsncigars.com/foundation-olmec-maduro-toro/ .

## U.S. Cohiba — not Cuban Cohiba
- Official https://www.cohiba.com/the-collection/red-dot/ confirms Toro Tubo 6 x 50, handcrafted Dominican Republic, Cameroon wrapper, Indonesian Jember binder, Dominican Piloto Cubano filler. Manufacturer's Cigar World confirms medium strength and nuts/cream/caramel: https://www.cigarworld.com/cigars/cohiba/red-dot/ .
- Official https://www.cohiba.com/the-collection/black/ confirms Gigante 6 x 60, CT Broadleaf, Dominican Piloto Cubano binder, Dominican/Mexican filler. Cigar World confirms handcrafted Dominican Republic and chocolate/coffee/nuts: https://www.cigarworld.com/cigars/cohiba/black/ . **Blend inconsistency**: Cigar World Black page incorrectly lists Broadleaf binder and omits Mexican filler; product's dedicated official Cohiba page takes precedence for blend. No precise factory disclosed. Manufacturer images show cigar plus tube and are labeled accordingly. Actual tube/vitola may differ.
