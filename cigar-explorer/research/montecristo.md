## Confirmed manufacturer profiles
- Classic Robusto **5 x 52**: https://www.altadisusa.com/cigars/montecristo/montecristo-classic-series/ confirms Connecticut Shade wrapper, Dominican binder/filler and Dominican Republic origin. Official page gives numeric strength 4 and describes smoother profile. Selected Robusto at https://www.casademontecristo.com/item/montecristo-classic/robusto/MXCROB.html confirms handmade Tabacalera de Garcia, parejo, mellow and cedar/nuts/coffee/cream. https://www.neptunecigar.com/cigars/montecristo-classic-robusto confirms handmade and round section. Uses mild strength matching mellow/mild retailer classification. Manufacturer only says Connecticut Shade; catalog does not add an assumed wrapper country.
- White Series Rothchilde **5 x 52**: https://www.altadisusa.com/cigars/montecristo/montecristo-white-series/ confirms Ecuadorian Connecticut Shade wrapper, Nicaraguan binder, Dominican and Nicaraguan filler, Dominican Republic origin and creaminess. https://www.neptunecigar.com/cigars/montecristo-white-rothchilde confirms handmade, round, mild and vanilla/nuts/spice/cream notes. **Source inconsistency**: Neptune specifications erroneously list USA Connecticut while its prose and official maker say Ecuadorian. Manufacturer's blend takes precedence. Factory confirmed in White Series release reporting https://cigar-coop.com/2025/03/altadis-u-s-a-to-add-montecristo-white-series-diadema-cigar-news.html . Mild-medium is a conservative descriptive classification; sellers differ from mild to medium. No more precise rolling town asserted.
- 1935 Anniversary Nicaragua No. 2 **6 1/8 x 52**: https://www.altadisusa.com/cigars/montecristo/montecristo-1935/ confirms all-Nicaraguan blend, Nicaragua manufacture and soft-pressed construction. The Altadis launch announcement quoted at https://www.cigaraficionado.com/article/montecristo-1935-anniversary-nicaragua-celebrates-85-years identifies San Lotano **Ocotal**, not Esteli. https://www.neptunecigar.com/cigars/montecristo-1935-anniversary-nicaragua-no-2 corroborates handmade and Ocotal factory. https://www.holts.com/cigars/all-cigar-brands/montecristo-1935-anniversary-nicaragua.html supplies medium-full and leather/red pepper/milk chocolate/earth/cedar tasting notes, labeled retailer. This product is regular black-band 1935 Anniversary Nicaragua, **not** Edicion Diamante or Doble Diamante.
